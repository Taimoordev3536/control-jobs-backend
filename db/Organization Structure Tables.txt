Organization Structure Tables 
10. cjobs_partners (Partners) 
• Partner organizations with commission structures 
• Key fields: Name, tax ID, address, commission %, retention % 
• Relationships: Parent to employers 
11. cjobs_tiposPartner (Partner Tiers) 
• Partner classification system 
• Values: 1=Gold (50%), 2=Silver (40%), 3=Bronze (30%), 4=Affiliates (30%) 
• Relationships: Determines partner commissions 
12. cjobs_empleadores (Employers) 
• Companies that hire workers 
• Key fields: Partner ID, name, tax ID, address, VAT rate 
• Relationships: Child of partners, parent to jobs 
13. cjobs_tiposEmpleador (Employer Types) 
• Employer classification 
• Values: 1=Home, 2=Static, 3=Remote 
• Relationships: Determines default rates 
14. cjobs_subtiposEmpleador (Employer Sub-Types) 
• Legal status classification 
• Values: 1=Individual, 2=Freelancer, 3=Company 
• Relationships: Used for invoicing rules 
15. cjobs_clientes (Clients) 
• Organizations receiving services 
• Key fields: Type, status, code, tax ID, address 
• Relationships: Linked to jobs and centers 
16. cjobs_centrosTrabajo (Work Centers) 
• Physical locations where work is performed 
• Key fields: Name, address, contact info 
• Relationships: Child of clients, parent to jobs
